Poha

Description

Poha, also known as flattened or puffed rice in some contexts, is a popular and light Indian food commonly eaten for breakfast. It is made by processing rice into thin, flattened flakes that can be quickly softened and cooked. Poha is especially popular in many parts of India and is often prepared with onions, mustard seeds, curry leaves, turmeric, green chilies, and peanuts. Its soft texture and mild flavour make it a simple yet delicious meal.

Poha can also be prepared in many different ways depending on regional preferences and personal taste. Vegetables such as potatoes, peas, and tomatoes can be added to make it more filling and nutritious. It is often garnished with fresh coriander and lemon juice, which give it a refreshing flavour. Because it is quick to prepare and easy to digest, Poha is a convenient and popular choice for breakfast or a light snack.

Ingredients

  • Rice
  • Onion
  • Potato
  • Green chilies
  • Peanuts
  • Mustard Seeds
  • Curry Leaves
  • Turmeric powder
  • Salt
  • Cooking oil
  • Lemon juice
  • Fresh coriander leaves
  • Green peas
  • Sugar (optional)

Steps

  1. Rinse the poha gently with water and drain it.
  2. Heat oil in a pan and add mustard seeds, curry leaves, and peanuts.
  3. Add chopped onions, green chilies, and potatoes, then cook until soft.
  4. Add turmeric and salt, then mix well.
  5. Add the soaked poha and mix gently.
  6. Cook for 2-3 minutes and garnish with coriander and lemon juice.
