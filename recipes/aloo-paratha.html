Aloo Paratha

Description

Aloo Paratha is a popular and delicious Indian flatbread stuffed with a spiced potato filling. It is commonly prepared for breakfast or lunch and is especially popular in North Indian cuisine. The outer layer is made from whole wheat dough, while the filling usually consists of mashed potatoes mixed with spices, green chilies, and fresh coriander.

Aloo Paratha is usually cooked on a hot pan with butter, ghee, or oil until it becomes golden and slightly crispy. It is commonly served hot with curd, pickle, butter, or chutney. Its flavourful filling and soft texture make it a filling and satisfying meal.

Ingredients

  • Whole wheat flour
  • Potatoes
  • Onion
  • Green chilies
  • Fresh coriander leaves
  • Salt
  • Red chili powder
  • Garam masala
  • Cooking oil or ghee
  • Water

Steps

  1. Boil and mash the potatoes in a bowl.
  2. Add onions, green chilies, coriander, salt, and spices to the mashed potatoes.
  3. Prepare a soft dough using whole wheat flour and water.
  4. Roll out a small portion of dough and place the potato filling in the centre.
  5. Seal the dough, roll it gently into a flat paratha, and cook it on a hot pan.
  6. Apply oil or ghee and cook both sides until golden brown.
